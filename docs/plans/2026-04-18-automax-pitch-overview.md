**Prepared for:** Chris, Auto Max

## The Short Version

We're building Auto Max a modern website with a built-in lead engine and an AI
assistant layer — designed to **feed your existing VinSolutions CRM**, not
replace it.

Your sales team keeps the tools they already use. Your new site just sends them
better leads, faster, with more context.

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Two issues, one fix.

1. **The current site looks dated and doesn't push visitors to act.** Traffic
   shows up, looks around, leaves.
2. **Leads that do come in aren't captured or qualified well.** No follow-up
   system, no attribution, no way to tell what's actually working.

---

## What You Get — Three Phases

Each phase is its own deliverable with its own price, its own timeline, and
its own "go / no-go" decision. You're not signing on for the whole thing up
front. You sign on for Phase 1 and decide from there.

### Phase 1 — Launch + Lead Pipe
**$3,500–5,000 one-time · $100–150/month**

- New site goes live (already ~70% built)
- Every form on the site — contact, test drive, financing, trade-in, vehicle
  search — feeds directly into VinSolutions the same way a Cars.com lead does
- You and your team see new leads in VinSolutions with **zero retraining**
- Instant SMS alert to you on every new lead
- A simple owner dashboard showing weekly lead count, source (which page did
  it come from), and which forms are working
- Password-protected until launch, then clean DNS cutover

**What you can say to a salesperson on day one:** *"Check VinSolutions. A lead
came in from the website five minutes ago."*

---

### Phase 2 — Live Inventory + Real Vehicle Pages
**$4,000–6,000 one-time · $50/month**

- New site pulls your live inventory from DEP every 15 minutes
- Every vehicle gets its own dedicated page with proper URLs, photos, and
  Google-friendly formatting
- "Ask about this vehicle" and "Schedule a test drive" buttons on every page
  automatically tag the VIN so your sales team knows exactly which car the
  lead is asking about
- Inventory stays in sync — sold cars drop off, new arrivals appear, no
  manual work

**What you can say:** *"Someone can find a specific car on Google, land on its
page, and message us about it — with the VIN already attached."*

---

### Phase 3 — AI Concierge
**$6,000–10,000 one-time · $200–400/month**

This is the part no off-the-shelf dealer platform gives you.

- **AI chat on your site** — customers can type "do you have a 4Runner under
  $25k with AWD" and get matched cars instantly, 24/7, from your live lot
- **Every lead gets pre-qualified by AI before it hits VinSolutions** — a
  short summary lands in the lead's notes: *"Budget around $22k, wants AWD,
  shopping this weekend, financing likely needed."* Your salespeople open a
  lead that's already triaged
- **AI drafts the first follow-up message** in your voice, sitting in the
  VinSolutions lead ready to paste-and-send. Cuts response time from minutes
  to seconds
- Analytics on how many leads the AI chat is producing and converting

**What you can say:** *"My leads show up pre-qualified, and my team's first
response goes out in under 60 seconds."*
